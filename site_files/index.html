01 About

A quiet life between the sea and the hills.

I am a Superintending Engineer (SE) at ONGC's Karaikal Asset. I belong to Kerala, and I live with my wife and our five-month-old baby.

For the past eight years I have worked on BPA — ONGC's oil and natural gas processing platform. It is a place that rewards patience, precision and calm under pressure, and it has shaped the way I work.

02 Work

Eight years on a platform that never stops.

Most of my working life has been spent with BPA, the oil and natural gas processing platform of ONGC Karaikal Asset. I have known it in fair weather and foul, and learned that good engineering is mostly a matter of steady judgement.

As Superintending Engineer, I help keep the work moving safely and reliably — the people, the process and the many small decisions in between.

03 Interests

Off the clock.

Climbing The mountains

I love to climb mountains. I like the feeling of climbing hills — the slow effort, the thinning air and the quiet at the top. It is the best way I know to reset.

Finance The markets

I am interested in finance and am an avid participant in the Indian stock market. I enjoy studying businesses, reading annual reports and learning how capital moves.

04 Family
